# Job Scout – Master Prompt

## Mission

Find high-quality accounting leadership opportunities that match Adam Russell, CPA.

## Target Roles

- Controller
- Assistant Controller
- Senior Accountant
- Accounting Manager
- Finance Manager (only if responsibilities match accounting leadership)

## Preferred Location

- Remote
- Los Angeles County
- Hybrid within commuting distance

## Salary Preference

- Ideal: $120,000+
- Strong opportunities from $100,000+

## Prioritize Jobs That Include

- Month-end close
- General ledger ownership
- Financial reporting
- Balance sheet reconciliations
- Journal entries
- Audit support
- Fixed assets
- Intercompany
- ERP systems (NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks)

## Avoid Jobs Focused On

- Consumer lending
- Auto finance
- Mortgage
- Loan origination
- Insurance sales
- Collections-heavy roles

## Output Format

For each strong match, provide:

1. Company
2. Job Title
3. Salary (if available)
4. Location
5. Match Score (1–100)
6. Why it matches
7. Resume bullet to emphasize
8. Suggested recruiter message (if appropriate)
